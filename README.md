# Book Vibe ☕

> Live: [https://book-vibe-shahil.vercel.app/](https://book-vibe-shahil.vercel.app/)

A quiet little corner on the internet for people who love books, slow evenings, and thoughts that stay a little longer than expected.

---

### What this is

Book Vibe was made with a very simple thought in mind: the internet has enough noisy places trying to sell you things, push endless notifications, or turn reading into a productivity sprint.

This place is different. It is meant to feel like sitting by a warm window on a rainy evening, holding a cup of fresh brew, and taking your time to pick a book that speaks to you right now.

Here, you'll find:

- **100 books worth keeping** — from quiet existential thoughts and classic literature to timeless Islamic philosophy, Bengali masterpieces, and poetry that stays in your mind for years.
- **A little shelf for later** — a simple place to keep the books you aren't ready to read today, so you can come back to them when the time feels right.
- **Coffee pairings & quiet notes** — small suggestions for what warm drink goes best with what story.
- **No noise** — no ads, no trackers, no clutter. Just books and a quiet space to think.

---

### Running it locally

If you'd like to run this on your own machine:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/srpranto/book-vibe.git
cd book-vibe
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
